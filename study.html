CNA Study Guide: Essential Skills

Review important nursing assistant skills before taking your CNA exam.

CNA Infection Prevention

  • Perform hand hygiene before and after patient care.
  • Wear appropriate personal protective equipment.
  • Dispose of contaminated materials properly.
  • Follow facility infection control procedures.

CNA Patient Safety

  • Keep the call light within reach.
  • Lock wheelchair brakes before transfers.
  • Follow the resident's care plan.
  • Report changes in condition to the nurse.

Resident Rights

  • Right to privacy.
  • Right to dignity and respect.
  • Right to refuse care.
  • Right to confidentiality.

CNA Medical Terminology

ADLs
Activities of daily living.
Ambulation
Walking or moving about.
Edema
Swelling caused by fluid accumulation.
Vital Signs
Measurements such as temperature, pulse, respirations and blood pressure.
